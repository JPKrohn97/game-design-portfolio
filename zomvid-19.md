      <h2>Game Overview</h2>
      <p>
        Zomvid-19 centers on the core fantasy of post-apocalyptic survival, where players establish an improvised defensive encampment amidst hostile infected territory. The experience challenges players to balance risk and security across daylight scavenging and nocturnal defense.
      </p>

      <div class="feature-box">
        <h4>Core Pillars</h4>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Fortify:</strong> Reinforce barricades, craft obstacles, and structure defensive choke points to funnel incoming threats.</li>
          <li><strong>Scavenge:</strong> Venture beyond the perimeter under tension to retrieve critical supplies, weapons, and repair parts.</li>
          <li><strong>Survive:</strong> Endure escalating waves of infected while managing weapon durability, ammunition, and defensive integrity.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
