      <h2>Game Overview</h2>
      <p>
        Developed as an assignment on boss design at Futuregames, <strong>I HATE FISH</strong> is a compact, high-intensity arcade combat experience designed to test player adaptability, spatial navigation, and execution in an underwater combat arena.
      </p>

      <h3>Design Problem</h3>
      <p>
        How to design a compact, adrenaline-fueled boss fight that balances multi-target crowd control with a readable, highly telegraphed colossus battle in full 3D space?
      </p>

      <h3>Design Solution</h3>
      <p>
        The encounter is structured into two distinct, complementary phases that transition player mindset from crowd management to single-target precision under pressure:
      </p>
      
      <div class="feature-box">
        <h4>Phase 1: "Shoot The Fish" (Mob Management & Warm-up)</h4>
        <p>
          Fight your way through an enclosed sunken arena filled with aggressive aquatic enemies. Players must stay constantly mobile, manage spatial positioning, and clear swift enemy waves before they swarm and overwhelm them. This phase establishes rhythm, movement mechanics, and weapon cadence.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="feature-box">
        <h4>Phase 2: "Face The Kraken" (Colossal Boss Fight)</h4>
        <p>
          Once the waters fall silent, a massive Kraken emerges from the abyss. The creature strikes with sweeping tentacle slams, testing player reaction times and dodging precision. Success requires learning its attack tells, evading lethal strikes, and exploiting exposed weak points while navigating the submerged architecture.
        </p>
      </div>

      <h3>Gameplay Loop</h3>
      <p>
        Arena surveillance → High-mobility kiting & mob elimination → Encounter escalation → Boss attack telegraph identification → Dodge / repositioning window → Precision fire at weak points → Victory.
      </p>

      <h3>Key Design Takeaways</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Telegraphing & Readability:</strong> Highlighting critical attack animations early so players can read impending tentacle strikes and react cleanly.</li>
        <li><strong>Arena Geometry:</strong> Utilizing ancient submerged pillars to break line-of-sight and provide tactical maneuvering options.</li>
        <li><strong>Pacing:</strong> Transitioning from high-frequency, low-threat mob targets to low-frequency, high-consequence boss patterns.</li>
      </ul>
