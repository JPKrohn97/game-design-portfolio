About the Game

Survive the  apocalypse by preparing your defenses during the day, to fight zombie hordes at night. Do you have what it takes to survive?

Key Features

Scavenge: Search the environment for randomized loot drops. Every run offers new survival opportunities.
Fortify: Build your own defense. Manually place furniture and fix them in place to create a strong barricade. Craft and set deadly traps.
Day & Night Cycle: Plan your strategy during the day, because the horde gets aggressive when the sun goes down.
Power Management: Keep the lights on! Manage your base's power supply to keep your defenses running through the dark.
