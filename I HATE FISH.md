      <h1 style="text-align: center;">I HATE FISH</h1>
      <h3 style="text-align: center;">A tiny platformer with big attitude</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Engine: Unity</li>
        <li>Role: solo designer/programmer</li>
        <li>Development time: 2 weeks</li>
        <li>Genre: platformer/comedy</li>
      </ul>
      <h2>Game overview</h2>
      <p>
        Players run, jump and use a small arsenal of comedic tools to avoid fish-shaped hazards and reach the coveted snack at the end of each level. The aesthetic is cartoony and fast-paced.
      </p>
      <h3>Core mechanics</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Precise platforming</li>
        <li>Temporary power-ups (vine swing, snack magnet)</li>
        <li>Environmental puzzles that subvert typical platforming conventions</li>
      </ul>
